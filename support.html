Frequently asked questions

Common questions before an enquiry.

Do I need a fully defined AI use case?

No. A clear decision, current workflow, users, and evidence are enough to begin. We can help structure the rest.

Can you work with our existing data and tools?

Potentially. The first step is to understand permissions, formats, interfaces, data ownership, and operating constraints before recommending an integration pattern.

Can you guarantee regulatory approval?

No. We help design and evidence a system for its intended use. Validation, approval, and compliance depend on the actual system, organisation, jurisdiction, and applicable requirements.

Do you offer a proof of concept?

A bounded pilot may be appropriate when the decision, evidence, success measures, and review path are sufficiently clear. We can discuss scope and controls before recommending one.

Where is my data hosted?

That depends on the proposed deployment. Shared, private-cloud, and customer-environment options have different controls. We document the chosen boundary before data is used.
