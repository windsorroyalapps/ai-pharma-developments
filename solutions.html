A practical rollout

Prove value without pretending the risk is zero.

A first workflow should be valuable enough to learn from and bounded enough to control. The operating evidence from that pilot becomes the basis for expansion.

01

Prioritise

Rank candidate decisions by value, feasibility, evidence readiness, and potential harm.

02

Baseline

Measure current time, quality, variation, and reviewer burden before introducing AI.

03

Pilot

Run a bounded workflow with representative users, cases, and review gates.

04

Decide

Expand, revise, pause, or retire the workflow using explicit acceptance evidence.
